My Story of Courage and Hope

Until the age of 32, my life felt stable and fulfilling. I had a loving wife, a wonderful son, and a career that was progressing well. Everything seemed to be moving in the right direction.

Then, without warning, my life changed. While at work, I began hearing voices that no one else could hear and seeing things that were not real. The voices constantly insulted me and frightened me by saying I should resign from my job or I would be arrested by the police. Overwhelmed with fear and confusion, I ran out of the office.

Concerned about my condition, my father encouraged me to consult a psychiatrist. After listening carefully to my experiences, the doctor diagnosed me with schizophrenia. Hearing that diagnosis was heartbreaking, and I felt as though my world had fallen apart.

My treatment began with medication. Although it helped manage my symptoms, I also experienced difficult side effects, including restlessness, anxiety, chest palpitations, insomnia, and several other challenges. Learning to live with both the illness and the side effects became part of my daily life.

At that time, I was working at TCS. Over the years, I continued building my career and later worked at Tech Mahindra, Wipro, and Infosys. Despite my ongoing psychiatric journey, I remained committed to my profession, adapted to every challenge, and continued to move forward with dignity and determination.

Today, my story is not just about living with schizophrenia—it is about resilience, hope, and refusing to let a diagnosis define who I am. Every step of my journey reminds me that courage is not the absence of struggle but the decision to keep moving forward despite it.
